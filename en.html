Flick Land Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-17 (earlier versions: 2026-10-16, 2026-09-30)

This is a translation of the Korean policy. If the two differ, the Korean policy applies.

itkong (the "developer") provides the mobile game Flick Land (package com.ponykit.flickland, the "app"). The app connects to the Ponykit Games game server run by the developer (the "service"). Personal information handled by the service follows the Ponykit Games Privacy Policy (in Korean); this page covers what applies to Flick Land only.

1. Summary

  • On first launch, a random account (a guest account) is created on the service without your name or contact details. You can link a Google account if you want.
  • Your nickname and character, gems, stones and skins, match records, missions, friends and rankings are stored on the service and decided by the service.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads. Rewarded ads play only when you press a button, and one banner ad shows at the bottom of the shop screen. When an ad shows, Google collects device information such as the advertising ID directly (section 5).
  • The app has no analytics or crash reporting tools other than ads. The developer does not sell personal information.
  • You can delete your account and data from the account deletion menu in the app's settings (in versions that have it) or by email (section 4).

2. Information stored on the service

The items, purposes, retention periods and processors are in sections 2–6 of the Ponykit Games Privacy Policy. For Flick Land they include:

  • Account information: a random account ID, login sessions, and the Google account identifier if you linked a Google account
  • Profile: nickname and chosen character
  • Game data: gem balance and its history, owned stones and skins, match results and the last match's state, mission progress and claimed rewards, ad rewards (the ad ticket, viewing time, payout result, and the match ID for a result-screen reward), and rankings
  • Friends: friend code, friend requests and friendships
  • Online matches: the room code and the moves exchanged during a match. The other player sees your nickname, character and stone design.

3. Information stored only on your device

Settings (sound, vibration, control sensitivity and so on), tutorial progress, a copy of recent match records, and the token that keeps you logged in are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the information on the device, but the account on the service is deleted only by the methods in section 4.

4. Deleting your account

  1. The "Delete account" menu in the app's settings (in versions that have it): the account is deleted right after you confirm.
  2. Email (ponykit@gmail.com): if you cannot use the app or your version has no menu, write "Delete account" in the subject and "Flick Land" and the friend code from the Friends screen in the body. We delete the account within 30 days of confirming and tell you the result.

What is deleted and what may remain is described under account deletion requests in the Ponykit Games Privacy Policy. A deleted account cannot be restored, and its gems, stones and skins are lost with it.

5. Ads

  • Rewarded ads play only when you press the ad button in the shop or the "Watch ad: gems" button on the match result screen; the service pays gems when you watch to the end. The service sets how many you can watch per day.
  • While the shop screen is open, one banner ad shows at the bottom of the screen. There are no full-screen ads during play or between screens.
  • Ads are served by Google LLC's Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To choose and show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraudulent clicks, Google collects device identifiers such as the advertising ID, the IP address (approximate location), app interactions such as ad views and clicks, and diagnostics directly from the device. The developer does not receive or store the advertising ID.
  • Where the law requires it, Google's consent screen appears before the first ad, and you can change your choice at any time under "Ad choices" in the app's settings. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in the device settings (Google › Ads).
  • For details, see section 7 of the Ponykit Games Privacy Policy and how Google uses information.

6. App permissions

  • Internet and network state: to connect to the service, play online matches and show ads.
  • Vibration: for game effects.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): lets the Google ads SDK read the advertising ID.

7. Children's information

The service is meant for users aged 13 and over, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated for ages 13 and up (PG) or lower.

8. Changes

When this policy changes, we post it on this page with its effective date. A change that adds collected items is posted before the update that brings that feature.
